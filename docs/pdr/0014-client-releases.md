# 0014 A feature reaches every client at once

- Status: accepted
- Level: project
- Date: 2026-11-27

## Decision

A feature reaches every client at once, so no client waits for the
slowest one.

## Rests on

- The owner's choice in an earlier session, recorded in #2 and #40.
- Assumption: every client draws the cards and the dashboards in one
  view language (PDR 0011, #20).

## Revisit when

The research of #20 chooses a view language that a client cannot draw.
