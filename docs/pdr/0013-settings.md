# 0013 Settings are a chat

- Status: accepted
- Level: project
- Date: 2026-11-27

## Decision

Settings are a chat: a pinned summary first, then cards with live
controls, for agents, workspaces and connectors alike. The look may
change when the UI is designed (#37).

## Rests on

- The owner's choice in an earlier session, recorded in #2 and #40.

## Revisit when

The UI work starts, and the first place of settings, the connection of
repositories (#37), is drawn.
